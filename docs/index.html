I’m Colt Williames, based in Omaha, Nebraska. My experience in financial-industry contact centers has shaped my interest in the connection between data, business decisions, and customer experience.

My portfolio brings together projects in classification, natural language processing, time-series analysis, recommendation systems, and deep learning. I work with Python and tools such as pandas and scikit-learn, and I have explored large language models, LoRA adaptation, and Streamlit application development.

I’m especially interested in financial analytics and using customer and operational data to ask clearer questions. I want my work to explain the problem, show the analytical choices, and make the findings understandable to someone who needs to act on them.
